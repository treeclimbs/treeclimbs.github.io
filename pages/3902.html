Summary:simul-locking symmetric gate lock system
Description & Commentary:

The locking mechanism consists of two spring loaded pivoting locking tabs on the gate, which fit into the notched section of the keylock nose. Both tabs must be depressed simultaneously for the gate to swing open.

The large HMS shape is very useful fitting many items such as eye-to-eye hitch cord + pulley.

I find this design to be one of the best locking mechanisms available. Triple action autolocking, but the two locks may be disengaged simultaneously and easily. The design is fully symmetric, operating exactly the same with the left or right hand. Because the tabs are somewhat recessed and located on opposite sides of the gate, it is far more secure against accidental opening than a standard lift-and-twist triple action carabiner.

I had been looking for one of these off and on for a few years after they became discontinued by Skylotec, but could only find them outside the US. During a trip to New Zealand, I walked into MetroGreen and the sales rep stated "that's the last one in country," only to learn I had in fact come all the way from the US to purchase it (or at least took a short detour from a lovely 2-week roadtrip). He also shared that these were popular with NZ tree climbers because the smooth gate and locking tabs permitted even tightly spliced rope eyes to slide over the gate rather than taking the long route around the carabiner. Big thank you to Hamish and the rest of the MetroGreen folks for chatting with us and making us feel so welcome in their wonderful country.
