Description & Commentary:

Classic carabiner inherited from Chouinard after the company transitioned to Black Diamond. This remained a staple utility carabiner in the early 1990s climbing, but fell out of favor as carabiners became more diverse in design (more asymmetric shapes, hotforging, keylock gates, etc) and specialized for different types of climbing.

This example is marked 22 kN for the major axis rated strength, which matches the photos Black Diamond catalogs from 1992 and 1993.

Before 1992, BD was advertising the Light D with a strength of 2300 kg. By 1996, BD advertised a Light D with a strength of 24 kN (if you have 1994 or 1995 BD catalogs, please contact me).
